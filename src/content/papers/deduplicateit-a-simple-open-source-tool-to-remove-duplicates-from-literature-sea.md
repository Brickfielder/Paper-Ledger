## Summary
This article describes deduplicate.it, a free web application designed to remove duplicate records from literature searches used in systematic reviews and meta-analyses. The authors start from the practical problem that searches across multiple databases often produce large amounts of overlapping records. They note that deduplication is a time-consuming step and is often underreported in review methods. The tool relies on normalized digital object identifiers (DOIs) and titles to identify duplicates. The authors emphasize that the approach is intentionally simple and transparent rather than opaque or heavily automated. When DOI information is missing, inconsistently formatted, or non-unique, the tool is designed to handle those cases carefully rather than assuming exact matches. The application is open access and hosted online at deduplicate.it. Its output includes exclusion files and a PRISMA-style flowchart in addition to the deduplicated record set. This is meant to make the process easier to inspect and document during a review. The paper reports a validation set drawn from five reviews in medicine, education, and social welfare. In that validation, deduplicate.it identified around 80% of duplicates and reduced the time needed for deduplication. The evidence presented is limited to a small validation set, so the paper mainly supports the tool as a practical workflow aid rather than as a definitive solution for all review settings.

## Why This Matters
Duplicate removal is a routine but often overlooked bottleneck in evidence synthesis. A simple, transparent tool that helps reviewers clean search results and document the process can save time and make systematic reviews easier to report.
## Caveats and Limitations
The validation described in the abstract is small and limited to five reviews, so the results may not generalize well to other disciplines, search strategies, or record formats. The tool appears to prioritize transparency and simplicity, which is useful, but that may also leave some duplicate cases unresolved compared with more complex methods. Because the article is abstract-only here, the details of the validation design, error cases, and comparisons with other deduplication tools are not available.
## Key Takeaways
- Deduplicate.it is a free web tool for removing duplicate records from literature searches.
- It uses normalized DOIs and titles to identify duplicates while handling imperfect DOI data.
- The workflow is designed to be transparent and easy to audit.
- It also outputs exclusion files and a PRISMA-style flowchart for reporting.
- In a small validation set, it found around 80% of duplicates and saved time.
